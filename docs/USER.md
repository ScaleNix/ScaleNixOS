## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Open your browser and go to your ScaleNix OS URL (e.g., `https://os.scalenix.fr`)
2. You'll be redirected to the login page
3. Enter your username and password
4. Click **Sign In**

### First Launch

On your first login, the **Onboarding Wizard** will guide you through the basics. You can:
- Step through each screen with **Next**
- Skip it entirely with **Skip**

The wizard won't appear again on future logins.

## The Desktop

Your desktop consists of:

- **Wallpaper** &mdash; customizable background image
- **Taskbar** &mdash; bottom bar with pinned apps, system tray, and clock
- **Widgets** &mdash; optional floating panels (clock, calendar, notes, etc.)
- **Desktop Icons** &mdash; shortcuts on the desktop surface

### Right-Click Menu

Right-click anywhere on the desktop to access:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Change Wallpaper** | Open wallpaper settings |
| **Add Widget** | Add a new widget to the desktop |
| **Spotlight Search** | Open the search overlay |
| **Toggle Theme** | Switch between dark and light mode |
| **Refresh** | Reload the desktop |
| **Lock Screen** | Lock your session |

## Window Management

Windows in ScaleNix OS work like a traditional desktop:

### Basic Actions

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Open** | Click an app in the Taskbar, Launcher, or Spotlight |
| **Move** | Drag the title bar |
| **Resize** | Drag any window edge or corner |
| **Minimize** | Click the yellow button (or click the taskbar icon) |
| **Maximize** | Click the green button (or double-click the title bar) |
| **Close** | Click the red button |
| **Focus** | Click anywhere on the window |

### Window Snapping

Drag a window to the screen edges to snap it:

| Drag To | Result |
|---------|--------|
| **Left edge** | Snap to left half |
| **Right edge** | Snap to right half |
| **Top edge** | Maximize |

You can also hover over the maximize button to see snap layout options.

### Picture-in-Picture (PiP)

Some windows support PiP mode &mdash; a small floating window that stays on top. Look for the PiP button in the title bar.

### Virtual Workspaces

Organize your windows across multiple virtual desktops. Use the workspace switcher in the taskbar or keyboard shortcuts to navigate between them.

## Taskbar

The taskbar is at the bottom of the screen and contains:

### Left Section
- **Applications** button &mdash; opens the full Launcher

### Center Section
- **Pinned Apps** &mdash; your favorite apps for quick access
- **Running Apps** &mdash; icons for currently open windows (click to focus/minimize)

### Right Section (System Tray)
- **Volume** &mdash; click to adjust system volume
- **Notifications** bell &mdash; click to open the Notification Center
- **Quick Settings** &mdash; Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, theme toggle
- **Calendar** &mdash; click the clock to view the calendar popup
- **User Menu** &mdash; your name and avatar (click for profile/logout)

---

## Launcher

Press the **Applications** button or hit **Meta+Space** to open the Launcher.

### Features

- **Grid View** &mdash; all available apps displayed as icons
- **Category Tabs** &mdash; filter by Communication, Productivity, Tools, Multimedia, System
- **Search** &mdash; type to filter apps instantly
- **Right-click** an app to pin/unpin it to the taskbar

### Pinning Apps

1. Open the Launcher
2. Right-click on an app
3. Select **Pin to Taskbar**

To unpin: right-click the pinned icon in the taskbar and select **Unpin**.

---

## Spotlight Search

Press **Ctrl+K** anywhere to open Spotlight &mdash; a quick search overlay.

- Type an app name to find and launch it instantly
- Results show app name, description, and category
- Press **Enter** to open the first result
- Press **Escape** to close

Widgets are small floating panels on your desktop that show useful information at a glance.

### Available Widgets

| Widget | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Clock** | Analog/digital clock with date |
| **Calendar** | Monthly calendar view |

| **Zimbra Calendar** | Upcoming events |

### Managing Widgets

- **Add**: Right-click desktop > Add Widget, or open the Widget Manager app
- **Move**: Drag the widget by its header
- **Resize**: Drag the widget edges
- **Remove**: Click the close button on the widget, or use Widget Manager
- **Configure**: Some widgets have settings accessible via a gear icon

## Notifications

### Notification Center

Click the bell icon in the system tray to open the Notification Center. Notifications are grouped by category:

- **System** &mdash; desktop and system events
- **Email** &mdash; new email alerts
- **Chat** &mdash; new messages
- **Calendar** &mdash; upcoming events

### Notification Sounds

Configure notification sounds in **Preferences** > **Notifications**, or use the **Audio** app.

## Tips & Tricks

### File Explorer Power Features
- **Drag & drop** files to upload them
- **Double-click** files to open them in the appropriate viewer (images, PDFs, videos, audio, markdown)
- **Right-click** for context menu (rename, delete, download, share)
- **Create documents** from templates (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) via the "+" button

### Multi-Window Workflow
- Snap two windows side-by-side by dragging them to screen edges
- Use **Alt+Tab** to quickly switch between apps
- Minimize windows by clicking their taskbar icon

### Quick App Access
- Pin your most-used apps to the taskbar for one-click access
- Use **Ctrl+K** (Spotlight) for the fastest way to launch any app

### Clipboard History
- Press **Ctrl+Shift+V** to access everything you've copied
- Click any item to re-copy it to your clipboard

### Screen Capture
- Open the **Capture** app to take screenshots or record your screen
- Screenshots are saved and can be annotated before saving

### Kanban Boards
- Create multiple boards for different projects
- Drag cards between columns
- Add labels and descriptions to cards
